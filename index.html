How It Works

From photo to
BBMP in seconds

No forms. No phone calls. Just open the app, take a photo, and let AI handle the rest.

01
📸
Photograph
Open the app and click a photo of any civic issue — pothole, garbage, sewage, or broken streetlight.
02
🤖
AI Detects
YOLOv8 model instantly identifies the issue type and assigns a severity score — Critical, High, or Medium.
03
📍
Add Location
Confirm your ward and address. The app auto-tags the correct BBMP department to route your complaint.
04
✅
Track It
Get a complaint ID. BBMP admin sees it on the live dashboard and updates the status as it gets resolved.
